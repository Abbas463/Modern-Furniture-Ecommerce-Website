Our Story

About Nordvik

Founded in Copenhagen, Nordvik represents the intersection of traditional Scandinavian craftsmanship and modern minimalist design. We believe that furniture should not only be beautiful but also meaningful—each piece tells a story of careful material selection, skilled artisanship, and thoughtful design.

Our partnership with master craftsmen across Denmark, Sweden, and Norway allows us to create furniture that honors time-honored techniques while embracing contemporary living. Every curve, every joint, every finish is considered with intention.

We source sustainable materials whenever possible, working with forests certified by the Forest Stewardship Council and using natural finishes that are safe for both the environment and your home.
